Three appellate division attorneys at Duane Morris received Chambers USA recognition: Benjamin Shatz in Los Angeles, Robert L. Byer in Pittsburgh, and Robert M. Palumbos in Philadelphia. The recognition reflects the firm's appellate depth across multiple federal and state jurisdictions on the East and West Coasts. Chambers USA rankings are determined through extensive research including client interviews and independent peer assessments.
