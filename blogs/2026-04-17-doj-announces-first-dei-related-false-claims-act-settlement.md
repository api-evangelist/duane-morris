On April 10, 2026, the U.S. Department of Justice announced its first False Claims Act settlement arising from allegations that an organization’s diversity, equity and inclusion programs constituted illegal discrimination. The $17 million settlement, announced by DOJ’s Civil Division, resolved allegations of unlawful DEI practices in connection with federal funding.
