An Arizona federal court decision clarifies jurisdictional transfer rules in class action litigation, confirming that 28 U.S.C. Section 1631 applies to personal jurisdiction defects. The ruling establishes that courts must transfer rather than dismiss cases when personal jurisdiction is lacking, providing defendants and plaintiffs clearer procedural guidance. This has significant implications for multi-defendant class actions where personal jurisdiction challenges arise.
