In bank holding company land, few structures offer the flexibility of a Qualified Family Partnership (QFP). Defined under section 2(o)(10) of the Bank Holding Company Act, a QFP allows family members to hold bank shares collectively without triggering BHC Act registration requirements. But such convenience comes with strict guardrails that require careful attention.
