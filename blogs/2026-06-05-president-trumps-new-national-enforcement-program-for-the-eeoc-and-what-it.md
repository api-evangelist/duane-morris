The EEOC announced its National Enforcement Plan for fiscal years 2025-2029, reflecting new priorities under the Trump administration and formally rescinding previous policies. The plan signals a shift in how the agency will prioritize and prosecute employment discrimination claims going forward. Employers should review their compliance programs in light of the updated enforcement focus areas.
