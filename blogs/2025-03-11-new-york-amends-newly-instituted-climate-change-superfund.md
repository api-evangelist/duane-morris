By Alicyn Craig, Louis C. Formisano and Matthew L. Capone On Friday, February 28, 2025, New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed into law Senate Bill 824 amending New York’s Climate Change Superfund Act (Act) just two months after the Act had itself been signed into law in December 2024.
