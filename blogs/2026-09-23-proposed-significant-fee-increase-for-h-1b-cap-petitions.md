Key Takeaway If adopted, the proposed $103,265-per-petition fee would represent a significant new cost for employers sponsoring cap-subject H-1B workers. Employers concerned about this additional cost should consider submitting a comment regarding the proposed fee. Public comments must be received by September 24, 2026, and must be submitted through regulations.gov under DHS Docket No.
