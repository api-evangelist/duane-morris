A June 29, 2026 New Jersey Appellate Division ruling in Martinez v. T. Slack Environmental Services addressed key issues in a wage-and-hour representative action brought under New Jersey wage statutes, confirming such claims can proceed without class certification but limiting the applicable look-back period for Wage and Hour Law and Earned Sick Leave Law claims.
