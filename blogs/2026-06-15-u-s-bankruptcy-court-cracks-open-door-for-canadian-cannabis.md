Cannabis companies have historically been excluded from U.S. bankruptcy proceedings because federal courts refused to administer assets tied to federally prohibited activity, leaving distressed operators with limited state-law options such as receiverships, out-of-court restructuring, or asset liquidation. A recent U.S. bankruptcy court ruling opens a path for Canadian cannabis restructurings.
