The court established boundaries regarding AI bias testing methodologies and disclosure obligations for applicant information in hiring systems in the Mobley v. Workday case. The ruling addresses how employers and HR technology vendors must handle AI-driven screening tools under anti-discrimination law. This decision is significant for employers using algorithmic hiring tools and the vendors that provide them.
