This post describes an incident where AI search results provided incorrect California arbitration appeal deadlines, demonstrating the dangers of relying on AI-generated legal information for procedural guidance. The author argues that lawyers using AI for legal research must independently verify all cited deadlines, rules, and case references. The post adds to a growing body of cautionary tales about AI hallucinations in legal practice.
