By Justin Mignogna and Gregory Bailey. The U.S. Senate Committee on the Judiciary met on Tuesday, December 17, 2024 to hold a hearing entitled “America’s High-Stakes Bet on Legalized Sports Gambling” in order to address the impact of the rapid expansion and proliferation of sports betting across the country.
