Duane Morris LLP is a Philadelphia-headquartered, full-service U.S. and international law firm founded in 1904 by Russell Duane, Steven Heckscher, and Roland S. Morris. Chaired by Matthew A. Taylor, the firm describes itself as "a law firm with more than 900 attorneys in offices across the United States and internationally" — with a U.S. footprint spanning the East Coast, Texas, the Mountain West, and California, plus international offices in London, Singapore, Vietnam (Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City), Shanghai, Myanmar, and joint ventures/alliances reaching Mexico and Latin America. An Am Law 100 firm since 2001, Duane Morris is known for depth in intellectual property (including patent prosecution and PTAB practice), trial and appellate litigation, real estate, corporate transactions, employment, healthcare, cannabis, and life sciences. The firm publishes an extensive LexBlog-powered thought-leadership network of more than 50 practice-area blogs, all of which expose RSS 2.0 feeds via the standard WordPress `/feed/` convention. Duane Morris does not expose any public developer API, SDK, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI spec, GraphQL endpoint, webhooks, status page, or developer portal; the GitHub organization at `github.com/duanemorris` exists but has no public repositories. The firm's only machine-readable surfaces are the RSS feeds of its blog network.

## APIs

Duane Morris LLP publishes **no public developer API**. There is no developer portal, SDK, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI specification, JSON Schema, GraphQL endpoint, webhooks, status page, or changelog. The GitHub organization at `https://github.com/duanemorris` exists but holds no public repositories.

The only machine-readable surfaces produced by the firm are the **RSS 2.0 feeds** exposed by its LexBlog blog network — more than 50 practice and industry blogs at `blogs.duanemorris.com` (plus the Duane Morris Institute at `duanemorrisinstitute.com`), each reachable via the standard WordPress `/feed/` convention. RSS validity has been spot-confirmed on the Class Action Defense, Cannabis Industry, Artificial Intelligence, and Duane Morris Institute feeds.

This repository profiles the firm itself — its practice areas, offices, leadership, and thought-leadership properties — rather than a developer-facing API surface.
